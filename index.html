Stock radarPattern scanner for Nifty 200 and US stocks

Today’s setups

Morning stars, hammers, breakouts and support bounces.

Hammer: long lower wick (at least twice the body, 60% of the range), small upper wick. Morning star: big red candle, small-bodied candle, then a green candle closing above the middle of the first. Near breakout: close within the “near” distance below the highest high of the lookback, or above it. Support bounce: one of the last 3 lows tested the lowest low of the lookback, and the latest candle closed green and above the previous close.

Price data

  • Easiest: run fetch_radar_data.py on your laptop. It saves 3 years of daily prices for the Nifty 200 (nse_daily.csv) and your US tickers (us_daily.csv). Load them here. The Nifty 200 list with sectors is already built in; load a newer list file only when NSE changes the index.
  • Also accepted: NSE bhavcopy files (old or new format, one file per day, load many at once), Yahoo Finance exports (one file per stock, named like RELIANCE.NS.csv), or any CSV with Date, Symbol, Open, High, Low, Close and Volume columns.
  • Weekly and monthly candles are built from the daily data, so load daily prices only. For monthly scans, keep at least 2 years.
  • Data you load is kept on this device.
